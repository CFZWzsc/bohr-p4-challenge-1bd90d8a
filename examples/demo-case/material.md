# Source note

Expression of GENEX was measured in five groups (n = 6 per group). Values are mean +/- SD.

KO was significantly lower than WT (p < 0.01), OE was significantly higher than WT (p < 0.01), and KD was significantly lower than WT (p < 0.05).

RES was significantly lower than OE (p < 0.05).
